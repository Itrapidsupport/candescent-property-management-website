Hinton sits on the edge of the Rockies, about an hour from Jasper, with a small-town pace and a lot of outdoor space. If you are planning a move here, a little preparation makes renting much smoother. Use this checklist as a starting point.

## Before you start looking

- **Set your budget.** Add up rent plus utilities, tenant insurance, internet and winter costs such as heating and snow tires. A common rule of thumb is to keep rent to about a third of your take-home pay.
- **Decide what matters most.** Number of bedrooms, parking, a garage for winter, laundry, pets, and distance to work or school.
- **Gather your documents.** Photo ID, proof of income, and contact details for past landlords and an employer reference. Having these ready means you can apply quickly when the right home comes up.

## When you view a home

- Check the heating system, windows and doors for drafts. Winters here are long, so a warm, well-sealed home matters.
- Ask which utilities are included and which you will set up in your own name.
- Look at parking and snow clearing. Who is responsible for shovelling walks and driveways?
- Ask about the lease term, the move-in date and whether pets are considered.

## When you apply

- Apply online and fill in every section. Incomplete applications take longer to review.
- Be upfront about pets, roommates and your move-in timing.
- Answer calls and emails promptly. Good homes in a small market can rent quickly.

## Before move-in day

- **Read the lease carefully.** Note the rent due date, how rent is paid, the notice rules and who handles what.
- **Arrange tenant insurance.** It protects your belongings and covers your liability. Many leases require it.
- **Set up utilities and internet** in your name if they are not included, so they are on when you arrive.
- **Book your move-in inspection.** In Alberta, the landlord and tenant inspect the home together and sign a written report. Go through each room slowly and note anything you see.

## Your first week

- Find the main water shut-off and the electrical panel.
- Save the office number and sign in to the tenant portal so you know how to pay rent and report maintenance.
- Test smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, and report any that do not work.
- Get to know the town: the Beaver Boardwalk, the recreation centre and the trails are great places to start.

Moving is a lot of small steps. Take them one at a time and your first Hinton winter will feel like home.
