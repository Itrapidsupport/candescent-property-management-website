Ask us a question

For renters

How do I apply for a rental?

Find a home you like on our Listings page, then apply online through our secure DoorLoop application. Applications are reviewed as part of our tenant screening process, and we will contact you about next steps.

What should I have ready before I apply?

It helps to have contact details for your current and previous landlords, your employment and income information, and government-issued photo ID close at hand. The application itself will tell you exactly what is required for that property.

How do I pay rent?

Rent is paid through the secure tenant portal. Once your lease is set up you will receive access, and you can sign in any time from the Portal Login link at the top of every page.

How do I report a maintenance issue?

Submit a maintenance request through the tenant portal, with photos if you can. Requests are reviewed and handled with trusted local vendors. For anything urgent, such as a water leak, no heat in winter or a safety concern, call our office right away at (587) 467-0570.

How much is the security deposit?

It depends on the property and is set out in your lease. Under Alberta's Residential Tenancies Act, a security deposit cannot be more than one month's rent, and it must be held in trust.

What happens at move-in and move-out inspections?

In Alberta, the landlord and tenant inspect the home together at move-in and again at move-out, and both sign a written inspection report. It records the condition of each room so there is a clear, fair record when the tenancy ends. Take your time, look closely, and note anything you see.

When do I get my deposit back?

Under Alberta rules, the deposit (with any interest owed) must be returned within 10 days after you give up possession of the home, or the landlord must provide a written statement of any deductions within that time.

Are pets allowed?

That depends on the individual property and its owner. Each listing notes whether pets are considered. If you have a pet, mention it on your application so we can confirm before you commit.

How much notice do I need to give to move out?

It depends on your lease. For a month-to-month tenancy in Alberta, a tenant generally gives at least one full tenancy month's written notice. A fixed-term lease normally ends on the date in the lease. Check your lease or ask us if you are unsure.

For property owners

What do you take care of for owners?

Our services cover tenant screening, rent collection, maintenance coordination with trusted vendors, and detailed financial reports each month. Tell us about your property and we will walk you through how we would manage it.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Candescent Property Management is a property management brokerage licensed by the Real Estate Council of Alberta (RECA).

How do you choose tenants?

Our comprehensive screening process is designed to find reliable tenants, reduce risk and support long tenancies. Screening is applied consistently and in line with Alberta's human rights and privacy rules.

How will I know how my property is doing?

You receive a detailed financial report every month, so you can see rent received, expenses and maintenance at a glance.

How much does property management cost?

Fees depend on the property and the services you need. Contact us for a quote based on your home.

Can I raise the rent?

Alberta does not cap the amount of a rent increase, but it does limit timing: for a periodic tenancy, rent can generally be increased only once every 12 months and only with written notice (three full tenancy months for a monthly tenancy). We handle the timing and notices for you.

This page is general information, not legal advice. For the full rules, see the Government of Alberta's information for landlords and tenants.
